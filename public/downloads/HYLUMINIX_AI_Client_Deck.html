Platform Architecture

Three-Layer Intelligent Automation System

Each layer operates independently but shares context, creating a unified automation pipeline from first contact to post-service follow-up.

💬
Layer 1 — Capture & Respond
  • Instant response to WhatsApp, web chat, and form submissions (<2 sec)
  • Natural language understanding for enquiries about pricing, availability, location, services
  • Multi-language support (English, Hindi, regional languages configurable)
  • Handles FAQ-type queries without human intervention
  • Image and document sharing (brochures, menus, price lists)
  • Missed call detection & automatic WhatsApp follow-up
  • Business hours awareness with customised out-of-hours messaging
📅
Layer 2 — Qualify & Convert
  • Smart lead qualification via conversational questionnaire
  • Intent scoring: hot / warm / cold lead classification
  • Calendar-integrated appointment booking with real-time slot availability
  • Multi-staff/multi-location slot management
  • Automated data collection (name, phone, email, service preference)
  • Lead routing to appropriate team member based on query type
  • Duplicate contact detection and conversation merging
🔔
Layer 3 — Retain & Re-engage
  • Automated appointment reminders (24h + 1h before)
  • Confirm / reschedule / cancel handling via WhatsApp replies
  • No-show re-engagement campaign (automated follow-up sequence)
  • Post-visit feedback & review collection
  • Repeat booking / reorder prompts based on service cycle
  • Seasonal & promotional campaign automation
  • Loyalty programme integration and milestone messaging
